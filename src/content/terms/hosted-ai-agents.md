A hosted AI agent is an agent that runs on the company's computers instead of yours. You sign up, connect your apps, and the company provides the model, the program that runs it, and a cloud computer with its own browser. You pay a subscription and maintain nothing, but your data and your limits are in someone else's hands.

## What "its own computer" means

An [agent](/terms/what-is-an-ai-agent-made-of/) needs a machine to run its tools on. Hosted agents get a **virtual machine**: a computer that exists only in a data centre, with a browser and files of its own.

That is why they keep working after you close your laptop. Anthropic says Claude Duet "has a virtual machine with its own browser, so it doesn't require your laptop or phone to be on." Manus describes itself as a colleague with its own computer that can browse, save files and install software.

Because the agent acts for you, approvals matter. Claude Duet's help page says it is designed to ask before it sends a message, makes a payment, signs a document or deletes anything.

## ChatGPT dots and OpenDot: same idea, opposite trade-offs

**ChatGPT dots**, launched by OpenAI in September 2026, are always-on agents with their own cloud computer and browser and more than 4,000 app plugins. **OpenDot** is a free, open-source desktop app built around the same idea: single-job assistants called Dots that watch your email, calendar and files, plus a coordinator called SuperDot.

| | ChatGPT dots | OpenDot |
|---|---|---|
| Who runs it | OpenAI's cloud | Your Mac or Windows PC |
| Price (Nov 2026) | First dot included in ChatGPT Pro ($100/month), Business Premium and Enterprise | Free (MIT licence); you pay your model provider, or nothing with a local model |
| Models | OpenAI's | Your choice: Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, Mistral, DeepSeek, local models |
| Your data | OpenAI's cloud | Plain files on your computer |
| Setup | Sign in | Build the app from GitHub, add your keys |
| Works while your computer is off | Yes | No |

Pick dots if you want zero setup and already pay for Pro. Pick OpenDot if you want your data at home and do not mind building an app.

## The others, by what they are best at

- **Claude Duet** (Anthropic): a proactive work assistant for professionals and small business owners. It sends a morning brief, flags what needs you in your inbox and calendar, finds unpaid invoices and drafts reminders. Research preview, waitlist only, for US Pro, Max and Ultra subscribers, inside your plan's usage limits.
- **Claude Cowork** (Anthropic): an agent for office work across your files and apps, with scheduled reports. It runs in the Claude desktop app on macOS, Windows and Linux (beta), and on web and mobile, on all paid Claude plans.
- **Meta Muse**: a consumer agent for everyday errands, in the Muse app and WhatsApp. Free with a weekly limit; Power $20/month and Maximum $100/month. Limited testing in the US.
- **Grok Bot** (xAI): a personal assistant with a persistent cloud computer, including desktop, files, terminal and browser, that hands coding jobs to Cursor. Included in The X Subscription Premium ($30/month), Super ($100) and Ultra ($200), but not in Plus ($8).
- **Manus**: built for big one-off jobs, such as researching a market and turning it into a report or website. Free plan with 300 daily credits; Pro from $20/month, paid in credits.
- **Perplexity Comet**: a web browser with an assistant that can act on pages for you. Free on Mac, Windows, iOS and Android.

The chat apps are moving the same way. ChatGPT Work has a cloud browser that keeps going after you leave, and Tasks in Gemini handles multi-step jobs, though Google lists it as unavailable in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK.

## What you pay for

Hosted agents come bundled with a subscription. You do not pay for the computer or the model separately, but you share one usage allowance. Claude Duet's background checks count toward the same limits as your chats, and Grok Bot shares a weekly pool with Grok and Cursor.

Prices above were checked on 20 November 2026 on each company's pages. They change often, so check the official page before you buy.

## The catches

- **Regions:** Claude Duet and Meta Muse are US-only for now.
- **Waitlists and previews:** "research preview" means features and limits can change.
- **Privacy:** the agent reads your mail and calendar on the company's servers. Read the safety page; Anthropic, for example, says Duet never sees your passwords or sign-in tokens.
- **Lock-in:** most hosted agents use only their own company's models.

## Sara's week with a hosted agent

Sara, a freelance designer, already pays for a Claude plan, so she joins the Duet waitlist. Once in, she connects Gmail and her calendar.

Each morning it lists the client emails that need her and drafts replies she approves with a tap. On Friday it finds two unpaid invoices and drafts polite reminders, and waits for her to say yes. She installs nothing and fixes nothing.

If she lived in Germany, Duet would not be open to her yet. A friend there might choose Claude Cowork, or OpenDot on her own laptop.

## Recap

- Hosted agents run on the company's cloud computer, so they work while your devices are off.
- You pay one subscription and maintain nothing, but you share usage limits and trust the company with your data.
- ChatGPT dots and OpenDot do the same job; dots is managed cloud, OpenDot is free software on your computer.
- Choose by job: Duet for proactive work, Cowork for office files, Muse for consumer errands, Grok Bot for errands with a full computer, Manus for big one-off tasks, Comet for the web.
- Check regions and current prices on the official pages; both change often.
